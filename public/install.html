Install Grade 3 Class Fund – MAEC

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this website in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The app icon appears on your home screen.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this website in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app at the top of the page if shown, or open the ⋮ menu and choose Install app / Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm. Open it from your home screen.

Installing is always your choice – nothing installs automatically. The app needs internet to show current balances, and signs you out after 14 days.
